❓ HOW IT ALL WORKS
🏁 The Basics

You run a fantasy team of 5 drivers — one per division (D0–D4) — plus a constructor, bought within your credit budget. Every real IMS round, your drivers earn the fantasy points they score in their division's race, and those points are your score for the round.

Your constructor earns its BOTT points from every driver racing for that team across all five divisions — so a strong constructor pick pays even when your drivers have a bad night.

⚡ Scoring — the fine print

Points land live. During a race weekend, results are imported division by division — your team's points tick up as each division comes in. The home page shows which divisions are already counted (the ✓ D0–D4 chips).

Reserve drives don't score fantasy points. If a driver races outside their own division (an off-division/reserve outing), those points count for their constructor's BOTT total only — not for your fantasy team.

Your team is snapshotted at lock. What you score with is the team you had when the round locked — transfers made afterwards count from the next round. The 📸 toggle on My Team lets you view last round's locked team and its points any time.

👑 Captains

Your captain scores 2× points each round. Pick one on My Team before lock.

Things people miss: if you don't log in, your previous captain carries forward automatically — and if you've never picked one, the app auto-captains your best season scorer, so you're never without the bonus. Selling your captain removes their armband for the upcoming round, so pick a new one.

🃏 Chips

🚀 Triple Captain — one round only, your captain scores 3× instead of 2×.

🃏 Wildcard — unlimited free team changes for one round. Rebuild the whole squad without burning banked transfers.

😇 Guardian Angel — pick any extra driver (any division, even one you don't own) and their points are added to your total for that round — a sixth scorer.

Each chip is once per season. Choose your moment.

💰 Prices — how the market moves

Prices, dominance and ELO price moves all settle once per round — after the D4 results come in (the last division to be imported). Earlier divisions' points land live, but nobody's price or dominance moves until D4 closes the round. Four forces move what a driver costs:

1. Dominance — how crushing their performance was versus their own division, compared to their previous race. The price shift is $0.1M for every 5 points of dominance change: beat your last showing and your price climbs, fall off and it drops. Big swings in form move the most money.

2. ELO — every race nudges price by their rating change. Rises are tiered: drivers under $15M climb fastest at roughly $1M per 30 ELO, easing smoothly to $1M per 50 ELO near the $30M cap — bargains climb quick, superstars don't snowball. Falls always bite at the full $1M-per-30 rate at any price, so overpriced drivers correct fast.

3. Non-finish penalty 💥 — every DNF, DNS or DSQ costs price, and repeat offenders pay more: the season ladder runs -$0.5M for your first, another -$0.5M for the second, -$1M for the third, and -$2M for every one after that. Crash-happy (or steward-bothering) drivers become cheap for a reason. The count resets every season, so everyone starts each season with a clean slate — non-finishes have been counting since Texas, Round 1 of Season 7.

4. Demand 🔥 — when 3+ teams own a driver, buying them costs a bit extra on top (shown in purple in the market). It's a flat surcharge based purely on how popular they are, not a percentage of their price — so a cheap fan-favourite and an expensive one pay the same premium at the same ownership. It grows as more teams pile in (and a little faster once 4+ own them), and unlike the forces above it isn't permanent: it fades if owners sell up. Popular picks cost a touch more; contrarian picks stay clean.

Driver prices live between $5M and $30M; constructors can reach $35M. Constructors are priced against their own season average — beat it and they rise. Tap any driver or constructor for their full 📈 season price chart, including your own buys and sells marked on the timeline.

🔄 Transfers

You get 1 free transfer per round, banked up to 3. Team changes beyond your banked transfers aren't allowed — plan ahead or save your Wildcard.

Swaps settle at the net price difference: sell a $10M driver, sign a $12M one, and it costs $2M of budget. Downgrades put credits back in your pocket — profit from price rises is real money for future signings.

🔀 Promotions & demotions

When the league moves a driver up or down a division, the fantasy market reacts the moment the roster is synced:

If you own them — they're automatically removed from your team (they no longer fit that division's slot) and refunded, with a notification sent so you know it happened. Cheap drivers (under $10M) refund their exact price so you're made whole; pricier drivers refund 75% of their value. You're then free to re-sign them in their new division, or spend the refund elsewhere.

Their price carries over to the new division, but their ELO baseline resets to the new division's standard — so a promoted driver is suddenly measured against tougher company, and a demoted one against easier. Expect their price to move accordingly over the next rounds: promotions often become bargains-in-waiting or overpriced strugglers, and spotting which is half the game.

⚠ Note that pricier drivers refund 75% of their value — owning a $20M star through a division move gets you $15M back, a 25% haircut. So if rumours of a move are swirling, selling first can still be the smart play.

📣 Fan Zones

Pledge yourself to a constructor and every fantasy point you score counts toward their Fan Zone total (leaderboard → 📣 Fan Zones). You wear their badge and colours on the leaderboard.

The pledge is locked for the whole season — no switching, no leaving until the new season resets everyone. Only rounds from when you join count; no retroactive credit. Your profile shows your share of the zone's points and whether you're carrying the zone… or being carried.

🎖 Badges glossary

🏆 won their division at the last race  ·  P pole position  ·  ⏱ FL fastest lap  ·  🧹 SLAM pole + win + fastest lap in one weekend.

On the leaderboard: ★ a team's current captain · constructor logo chips show each player's Fan Zone · row colours are Fan Zone colours.

📅 The weekend cycle

1. Race week opens — make transfers, set your captain, watch the countdown. 2. Teams lock at the deadline (your line-up is snapshotted). 3. The weekend goes 🔴 LIVE and points land across two race days: D0 & D1 are imported together on the first day, then D2, D3 & D4 together on the second — your team's points tick up as each batch comes in. 4. Once D4 lands and the round is complete, prices move and everything settles. 5. Once the previous round's verdicts are settled, editing reopens — you bank a fresh free transfer and the next countdown begins.

Miss a deadline? Your locked team races as-is, captain included — you always score.

Fills in captains for any team that has none set for a race — carrying forward their previous captain, or auto-assigning their best season scorer if they've never picked one. 📸 Refresh Race Snapshot

Re-takes a race's team snapshots from CURRENT team selections — use when a race's snapshot missed late signings (e.g. wildcard transfers made after the race doc was created). ⚠ Only safe while teams haven't changed since that race — i.e. during or right after its round.

🧹 Fix Duplicate Race

When the same round got imported under two different names (e.g. "Spa Francorchamps" and "Spa"), you end up with two race docs. Pick the one to KEEP and the duplicate to DELETE. This removes the duplicate's race document only — it does not undo any price/ELO/DNF changes that import applied. ⚠ After deleting, re-run pricing if the duplicate double-counted (see note below).

💥 Rebuild DNF Records

Recomputes every driver's season non-finish count and DNF price penalties from the race documents that currently exist. Use this after deleting a duplicate race to clear phantom DNFs — real non-finishes are kept, stray ones (from the deleted import) are removed, and the price penalty is corrected (refunding any over-charge). Safe to run any time; it reconciles rather than double-applies.

🐞 Bug Reports
🗑 Purge Phantom Driver

For entries that aren't real drivers (bad rows, test entries, duplicates with no real counterpart). Strips their points, DNFs and team history from every race document — so constructor totals and standings correct themselves — then deletes the driver. Any fantasy team fielding them has the slot cleared. ⚠ Permanent. If they're a duplicate of a REAL driver, use Merge below instead so the points are kept.

🔀 Merge Duplicate Drivers

When one real driver was entered under two names (e.g. coldpalmer & HPjones), merge them. All of the duplicate's race results, points, DNFs, team history and fantasy-team ownership are reassigned to the driver you keep, then the duplicate is deleted. ⚠ Permanent — run a data backup first if you can. Prices/ELO aren't recombined; the kept driver's own price stands (re-import or replay if you need it recomputed).
